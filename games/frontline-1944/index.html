Field Manual

FM 7-10 · Rifle Company, Infantry Regiment (abridged)

The objective

Each battle is fought along a line of trenches. Your command post is on the left and the enemy's is on the right. Occupy the enemy command post with no defenders left inside to win. If they take yours, you lose.

Deploying

Supply comes in steadily. Spend it on squads with the cards at the bottom, or keys 1–8. New squads march up to your front-line trench. Each kill adds a little supply and command.

Orders

Click a soldier to select his squad (Shift adds more), or click a trench to select everyone in it. With nothing selected, orders go to every squad.
F Advance one trench · G Assault all the way · H Hold the nearest trench · R Fall back.

Cover and morale

Soldiers in a trench are hard to hit. Bunkers are harder. Men in the open under heavy fire get pinned, then break and run. Machine guns pin, mortars and artillery dig defenders out, and smoke blinds the enemy while you cross. Assault squads throw grenades and win fights inside trenches.

Armor

Rifles barely scratch a tank. Use bazookas, your own armor or an air strike. Tanks can't take trenches alone, because only infantry can clear and hold them.

Fire support

Spend command points with Z X C V, then click the battlefield to aim. Esc or right-click cancels.

Camera

Drag the battlefield, use A/D or the arrow keys, scroll, or click the strip map at the top. Space pauses and T toggles double speed.
